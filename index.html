        How QCKCUT works

        Sources

        Drop video or audio files anywhere on the page, or press + in the Sources panel. Audio is a source like any other: it gets a waveform instead of a filmstrip, and it can be clipped and put on the sequence, where it plays over black. Everything stays on your machine: nothing is uploaded, and the project is saved in this browser so it is still here when you come back.

        Clips

        Scrub to the start of a moment and press C. Scrub to the end and press C again to keep it as a clip; Esc cancels. The selection follows the playhead while you are marking, so you see the clip you are about to make. I and O are still there for adjusting a range by hand.

        A clip is only a reference to a start and end time in its source, so making one costs nothing and deletes nothing. Click a clip to select it and type exact times into the boxes that appear, or drag the handles on the filmstrip. Every clip on the current source shows as a band along the bottom of the filmstrip.

        Sequence

        Drag clips or whole sources onto the Sequence track to assemble them, and drag them within the track to reorder. Position comes from order, so there are never gaps to tidy up. Clicking an item jumps the preview to its first frame. Play sequence plays the whole thing across the cuts, and Export sequence renders it to one MP4 at the first item's dimensions, letterboxing anything shaped differently.

        Sound

        The preview plays audio, and dragging the filmstrip plays a short grain at the playhead so you can hear where you are. M mutes it. The music bed plays under the sequence and is mixed into the exported file at whatever level you set, trimmed to the sequence length. Drop a file, or drag a source, onto the waveform lane under the sequence to set it.

        Export

        Export renders the current range to an MP4 and downloads it, keeping the audio. Encoding runs on your GPU, so it is usually several times faster than the clip is long.
