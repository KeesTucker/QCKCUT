      How QCKCUT works

      Sources

      Drop video files anywhere on the page, or press + in the Sources panel. Everything stays on your machine: nothing is uploaded, and the project is saved in this browser so it is still here when you come back.

      Clips

      Scrub the filmstrip to find a moment, set the range with I and O, then press C to keep it as a clip. A clip is only a reference to a start and end time in its source, so making one costs nothing and deletes nothing. Click a clip to select it and type exact times into the boxes that appear, or drag the handles on the filmstrip. Every clip on the current source shows as a band along the bottom of the filmstrip.

      Export

      Export renders the current range to an MP4 and downloads it, keeping the audio. Encoding runs on your GPU, so it is usually several times faster than the clip is long.
